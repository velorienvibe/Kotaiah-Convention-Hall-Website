KOTIAH CONVENTION HALL — WEBSITE CONCEPT

Run locally:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open this folder in VS Code.
3. Right-click index.html and choose Open with Live Server.

Pages: index.html, about.html, facilities.html, gallery.html, contact.html
Assets: style.css, script.js, images/

Important concept-demo notes:
- Venue contact, address, rating and review count were sourced from public business listings. Confirm all information with management before launch.
- Public listings disagree on capacity and some facility details, so the demo intentionally avoids a fixed capacity figure. Public directory listings mention air conditioning, a stage, guest/bridal rooms and power backup; availability must be confirmed directly.
- Three preview image URLs point to photographs displayed in the public Justdial listing. The site automatically switches to local SVG concept visuals if those external images are unavailable. The SVGs are illustrations, not photographs of the venue. Obtain owner/platform permission before using any third-party photo commercially.
- WhatsApp enquiry opens a prefilled message to the publicly listed number +91 99895 85599. Confirm the correct booking contact with management. The form does not store data.
- Google Fonts and the embedded Google Map require an internet connection.
